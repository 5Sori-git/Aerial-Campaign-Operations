<size=120%><b>2026-11-21</b></size>

<b>NEW: PILOT LEVEL AND TITLES</b>
• Your pilot level is the total of your upgrade steps. It shows on the high score board and a tag above your head for other players
• The tag above your head shows your title, level and callsign. It is visible within 25 m, hides while you sit in an aircraft, and can be turned off with PILOT TAGS under General in settings
• Added a pilot record board on the crew room lockers (next to the update notes): see your clears, five-phase clears, kills, flight time and CIC records
• Earn 78 titles from your records. Max out an upgrade category for specialty titles like Fighter, Attack and Bomber Pilot, and every named ace and giant boss has Hunter, Conqueror, Executioner and Strike Team titles, and bosses you have not met yet stay hidden
• Drag the board's list to browse and tap an earned title to wear it (tap again to remove). Your title shows on your pilot tag with an icon whose color marks its tier

<b>NEW: F/A-18F TWO-SEATER</b>
• Use the aircraft button under the icon on your briefing room roster card to switch your slot between the single-seat F/A-18E and the two-seat F/A-18F. Weapons, upgrades and decals stay the same. The type is locked during the launch countdown
• Once an F/A-18F has launched, players who are not on the roster can tap its card to ride in the back seat, even in flight. Roster pilots can't ride in another aircraft, and you can't join the roster while sitting in a back seat
• Back-seat displays: tactical map with legend in the center, RWR (missile and lock directions) on the left, mission status on the right, flight data (speed, altitude, climb rate, heading) on top and pilot info at the lower left. Only the back-seat rider sees them
• Leaving the back seat in the air or away from the carrier returns you to the briefing room. The back-seat passenger is also returned to the briefing room when the aircraft type changes or the aircraft is recovered or destroyed
• Damage is applied once per hit, judged by the pilot, even with two aboard

<b>BRIEFING AND HANGAR</b>
• Roster cards are cleaned up: the callsign shows next to the slot number, pilot, status and loadout are left-aligned, and a launched F shows who is in the back seat
• The hangar panel sortie roster shows callsigns and nicknames
• In the shop's airframe tab you can now lower the applied level of engine and mobility one step at a time (−, +, MAX left of the buy button). Your purchased upgrades and pilot level stay the same, and the change applies from your next boarding. Use it if the aircraft feels too twitchy
• The spectator camera and CIC goggles show each slot's current aircraft type (F/A-18E or F/A-18F)
• The decal studio has an F/A-18E / F/A-18F button at the right end of the tab row to switch the preview aircraft (preview only, your sortie aircraft type is set on the roster card)
