<b>CIC</b>
• New CIC tools: during an operation, point at the map on the CIC tactical table and pull the trigger (click) to mark a spot with a light beam. Then pick a tool on the panel beside the table
• Waypoint: the marked coordinates show on every pilot's HUD for 30 seconds
• Recon UAV: circles level above the marked coordinates and identifies vehicles below it in Urban Strike. It can't be shot down
• Wingmen: two friendly AI fighters patrol the area around the marked coordinates. Kills count for whoever called them
• Cruise missiles: the friendly cruiser fires 4 cruise missiles around the marked coordinates (cooldown shared by everyone). Each is weak but they spread out, and enemy air defenses aim at the missiles first, so they shoot less at pilots meanwhile. Enemy air defenses can shoot them down
• Cruise missiles in flight show as LACM on pilot HUDs and as blue dots on the CIC table. When they finish, the panel shows how many hit and how many were shot down
• Wingmen and cruise missiles ask you to confirm or cancel first (cancels itself after 10 seconds)
• First-person goggles: bring the goggles on the CIC desk to your face (desktop: just pick them up) to watch from the recon UAV or another pilot's aircraft. Pick what to watch on the monitor next to them
• In the goggles' UAV view, line up the center crosshair and pull the trigger (click) to mark a spot directly. Switch picture quality between HD and FHD on the goggles monitor (local)
• CIC tools now have radio calls: waypoints (datalink coordinates received), cruise missile launches and wingmen are heard by everyone, and the recon UAV call only by whoever sent it
• Players who only used the CIC tools without flying get 20% of the reward
